## Normative accessibility

- W3C WCAG 2.2 — https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/
- WAI-ARIA standards overview — https://www.w3.org/WAI/standards-guidelines/aria/

## Accessible interaction patterns

- WAI-ARIA Authoring Practices Guide — https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/

APG is guidance/pattern evidence, not a normative standard and not a complete UI Design System.

## Apple platform experience

- Apple Human Interface Guidelines — https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/
- Apple branding guidance — https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/branding
- Apple layout guidance — https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/layout
- Apple accessibility — https://developer.apple.com/accessibility/

Use for native platform familiarity, adaptive layout, accessibility and brand/platform fit. Apple guidance does not define BThwani brand or Product workflow.

## Android and Material experience

- Android user-experience quality guidance — https://developer.android.com/quality/user-experience
- Android core/adaptive app quality — https://developer.android.com/develop/adaptive-apps/quality-guidelines/core-app-quality
- Android accessibility — https://developer.android.com/guide/topics/ui/accessibility
- Material Design 3 — https://m3.material.io/

Use for Android-native interaction, adaptive behavior, accessibility and Material component semantics. Material is an implementation/design reference, not BThwani visual authority.

## Design-token interoperability

- Design Tokens Community Group technical reports — https://www.designtokens.org/technical-reports/
- DTCG stable 2025.10 report — https://www.designtokens.org/tr/2025.10/

DTCG 2025.10 is a stable Community Group report and is not a W3C Standard. Adopt its interchange format only when it materially improves cross-tool/platform interoperability.
